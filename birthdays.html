Your Birthday

Your Night Your Disco

Your birthday only comes around once a year—celebrate it under the disco ball at Windsor’s ultimate throwback party destination.

Whether you’re planning a night out with a few friends or a full birthday celebration, Disco Inferno is the perfect place to dance, sing, and make memories all night long.

Reserve a birthday booth, enjoy VIP treatment, and let our team help make your celebration one to remember. From retro hits and signature cocktails to packed dance floors and unforgettable photo moments, we’ll take care of the details so you can focus on having fun.

Gather your crew, pick your date, and get ready for a birthday party that’s anything but ordinary.
