Before You Arrive

Things To Know

DISCO INFERNO IS A 19+ VENUE LOCATED IN THE HEART OF DOWNTOWN WINDSOR

Dress to impress! Retro outfits, fun costumes, and vintage vibes are always encouraged — it’s part of what makes Disco Inferno Windsor’s ultimate throwback bar.

To make the most of your night out, we highly recommend purchasing advanced tickets online for every event. This helps you skip the long lines and guarantees your spot on the dance floor.

While we love music requests, please note that Disco Inferno is a throwback nightclub dedicated to the biggest hits from the 70’s, 80’s, 90’s, 2000’s, and 2010’s. We don’t play current Top 40 songs released after 2010, unless it’s part of a special themed event.

Please be aware that we strive to provide a fun and exciting environment for patrons of all ages.

There are a few rules that we need to enforce including:

  • Absolutely no "free-pouring" with bottle service.
  • Vaping is not permitted inside the venue.
Style Code

The dress code

To maintain a fun, stylish, and safe environment, all guests are expected to follow our dress code.

Not Permitted:

  • Athletic wear: Jerseys, sweatpants, gym shorts, or workout gear
  • Footwear: Flip flops, slides, or beach sandals
  • Excessively baggy or ripped clothing.
  • Work boots (unless approved by management)
  • Tank tops (for men)

Encouraged:

  • Fashion-forward, trendy, or nightlife-appropriate attire. Smart casual or upscale casual wear
  • Clean sneakers, dress shoes, or fashionable boots

Notes:

Management reserves the right to deny entry based on attire. Exceptions may be made for themed or special events.
