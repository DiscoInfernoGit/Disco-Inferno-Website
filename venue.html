The Dance Floor

Ignite your inner groove

Prepare to boogie the night away on Windsor's first LED Disco Dance floor, surrounded by state-of-the-art sound and lighting. Let the vibrant hues and shimmering patterns ignite your inner groove, as our top-tier DJs curate an eclectic mix of disco classics and today's hottest tracks.

Indulge in our signature retro cocktails and a thoughtfully curated wine selection, perfectly crafted to complement the energetic atmosphere.

Disco Inferno is open every Friday and Saturday night, ensuring your weekends are filled with unforgettable moments.

Music Playlists

A high-energy journey through the decades

Come dance to a night of groovy music with our nightly playlists at Disco Inferno! We're always open to taking requests by texting 519-791-3496!

Disco Inferno is more than just a disco bar — we’re Windsor’s ultimate throwback nightclub. Known as the best dance club in Windsor, Ontario, our music format is designed to take you on a high-energy journey through the decades.

Early in the night, we set the tone with 70’s disco classics, 80’s hits, and 90’s throwback anthems that bring the nostalgia alive. As the night progresses, the energy builds with the biggest songs from the early 2000’s, leading into non-stop 2000’s and 2010’s party hits that keep Windsor’s nightlife crowd on the dance floor until last call.

Whether you’re looking for the best dance club experience in Windsor or just want to sing along to your favorite throwback songs, Disco Inferno is the Windsor throwback bar where every generation’s top hits come together under one roof.
